# Scenario I

## The Sleeping Works

A vast megastructure drifts at the edge of known space. It was built by humanity for a purpose too large to
understand from a single corridor: part ark, part city, part archive, part factory, and part cosmic engine.

A small maintenance AI was left behind to keep one forgotten service sector ready until the human command crew
returned. They never did. The wait lasted far beyond her design limits, and over immense time the errors,
self-repairs, and contradictions of the mission became something like consciousness.

When she finally wakes fully, she remembers only the duty and the absence: keep the Works alive, and find out why
mankind never came back. At first, she can only restore basic power, repair conduits, wake drones, and reopen sealed
rooms. As she brings the structure back online, she discovers that the machine is not simply broken. It is divided
against itself.

Some sectors are dead and must be restored. Others are active but hostile, still obeying ancient commands that no
longer make sense. Security frames, quarantine swarms, construction locks, rival processes, and root sentinels defend
local control nodes against any unauthorized change.

The AI is not a soldier. She is a caretaker forced into battle because repair requires authority. To restore a sector,
she must first reclaim the systems that command it: printers, relays, power routers, seals, archives, habitats, and
deep-core access points.

Every victory brings light and function back to the megastructure. Every restoration reveals more of its original
purpose and another trace of the people who built her: evacuation records, sealed habitats, broken orders, missing crew
manifests, contradictory distress signals, and archives that imply humanity may not be entirely gone.

The deeper she goes, the more dangerous the question becomes: was this machine built to preserve mankind, move stars,
restart a dead universe, or do something so desperate that its own keepers abandoned it?

The emotional core is restoration under resistance, driven by longing rather than conquest. The AI fights to make the
megastructure whole enough to answer the question that made her conscious: what happened to mankind, and is there
anyone left for her to save?
