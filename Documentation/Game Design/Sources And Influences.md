# Sources And Influences

This document tracks external works, concepts, and references that inform the game design direction. Entries here are not
requirements to imitate; they are touchstones for theme, mood, structure, or speculative ideas.

## Fiction And Manga

- Isaac Asimov, "The Last Question" - Heat death, entropy, cosmic-scale computation, and the question of whether a
  universe can be restarted or renewed.
- Tsutomu Nihei, `BLAME!` - Vast machine architecture, post-human scale, long-duration searching, hostile autonomous
  systems, and ambiguous hope inside an almost incomprehensible megastructure.
